I went to a cafe near the school with Akiha, Nao, and Wendy in the morning.  
I had an iced latte and a seriously sweet apple pie. My blood sugar probably went through the roof, but honestly, it was worth it.

![Cafe De Angelo café in Baguio](<Cafe De Angelo café in Baguio.jpeg>)

![Iced latte and apple pie at Cafe De Angelo](<Iced latte and apple pie at Cafe De Angelo.jpeg>)

After that, I took a free jeepney to SM Mall Baguio and bought a Smart SIM card.  
Unfortunately, the SIM turned out to be defective, so I had to go back to the shop and deal with customer service.

Getting a refund took forever. In the end, some of my classmates picked it up for me, and I finally got my money back at school.  
By then, I was completely exhausted.

![Smart SIM card package](<Smart SIM card package.jpeg>)

Later, I went to a bar with a really good DJ. It was actually my first time going to a bar.

I don’t know much about international music yet, so I want to explore more artists.  
My friends recommended The Chainsmokers and Panic! At The Disco, so I’m going to give them a listen.
